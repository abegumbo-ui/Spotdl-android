## SpotDL Downloader

Paste a YouTube Music, YouTube or Spotify link, press **Go**, and the songs are
saved to `SpotDL Downloader` in your phone's internal storage.

### Install
1. Download **SpotDL-Downloader-*.apk** below on your phone.
2. Open it and allow "install from unknown sources" if Android asks.
3. Open the app and allow **All files access** when asked.

If you installed an earlier test build, uninstall it once first. From this
release on, updates install over the top.

### What it does
- MP3 (converted on the phone), M4A or Opus, with album art and tags
- Only YouTube Music audio tracks are ever downloaded, never video
- Works in the background; any size playlist; failed songs can be retried
- Search Spotify and YouTube Music for songs and whole artists, pick the cover you like
- A PDF report of what downloaded and what failed
- A dark, rounded look with its own icon and loading screen
- Titles in Hebrew, Spanish, Russian, Greek, Arabic and more show their real letters
- Play button on every search result to check you have the right song, and a song
  card (tap the title) with cover, artist, album, length and the song that would download
- **New in 1.8:** a Library for everything you downloaded (songs, albums, artists,
  playlists, favorites, search and sorting), a queue with Play next / Add to queue,
  a full Now Playing screen with shuffle, repeat and a sleep timer, and the Android
  back button

For 64-bit Android phones (Android 7.0 and newer).
